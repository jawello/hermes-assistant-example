# Cyprus shipping & returns

When something needs to go out from Cyprus (a return, warranty/RMA, selling
gear, sending documents), produce a complete errand task: the right courier
drop-off, its address + Google Maps point + hours, and what to take.

## Couriers on Cyprus

- **UPS** in Cyprus is run by **G.A.P. Vassilopoulos Group**, trading as
  **G.A.P. AKIS Express** — the authorized service contractor. The physical
  drop-off is a G.A.P. AKIS Express store/hub, not a "UPS"-branded shop.
- Hubs exist in Limassol, Larnaca and Nicosia, plus agent points; pick the one
  nearest the owner's home (from memory) and read its hours on the day.
- Full store list + hours: https://gapakisexpress.com/stores/
- Official UPS Cyprus locations: https://www.ups.com/cy/en/locations

## Finding a location + coordinates (recipe)

1. Hit the organisation's own "locations / branches / stores" page first — it
   carries the authoritative address and hours (UPS locator, gapakisexpress.com/stores).
2. For coordinates, look the street address up on mapcarta.com (lat/long + plus
   code), geocords.com, or 2GIS Cyprus.
3. Build the Google Maps link yourself: `https://www.google.com/maps?q=<lat>,<lng>`
   — don't wait for a shortened `maps.app.goo.gl` link.

## International RMA return prep (what to take)

A reusable checklist from a manufacturer return:
- The item to ship — and ONLY what the RMA form lists.
- RMA form (printed from the vendor email).
- Prepaid shipping label (printed).
- Commercial invoice (printed; some countries want multiple copies on the box
  exterior — UK requires 3).
- A box; write the RMA number on the outside.
- Get a drop-off receipt at the counter.

Prepaid-label returns need printing: flag "print first, not at the office"
whenever a label/form is involved, early enough to arrange it.
