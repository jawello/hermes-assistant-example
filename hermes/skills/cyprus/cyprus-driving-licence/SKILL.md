# Cyprus Driving Licence (RTD / TOM)

Use when asked to book, exchange, renew or replace a driving licence
(водительское удостоверение / ВУ) on Cyprus.

## Key fact: TOM has no own calendar — but KEP/CSC has online booking

The Road Transport Department (RTD / ΤΟΜ) does **not** publish appointment
windows on its own site; direct TOM booking is email/phone only (see below).
**However**, driving-licence applications (issuance, exchange, replacement)
can be booked **online** through the Citizen Service Centre (ΚΕΠ / CSC)
platform — the self-service route. See next section.

## Online booking via KEP / CSC (preferred)

URL: `https://kep-kepo.gov.cy/appointments` (gov.cy entry point:
`gov.cy/service/ilektroniki-diefthetisi-rantevou-sta-kentra-politi-ke-po-kypriaka-tachydromia`).

Flow: pick service type → `Road Transport Department – Issuance of Driving
License` → pick the service point (the CSC of the owner's district) → pick
date/time → enter name, ID number, email, mobile → get an **appointment code**
(two groups of four letters) plus a QR. On the day, enter the code at the desk
screen or scan the QR to print a ticket.

- Fee €40 (65+ free, **except** reissue due to loss — then €40 applies).
- For a ready-for-collection SMS, read the named collection office and any surrender requirement literally; collect there rather than assuming the application office. No appointment is needed to *collect* a finished document (ID / passport / licence) — only to submit an application (official gov.cy appointment page). Bring the old licence when the SMS explicitly requires it, plus identification and the SMS. Verify the local public-holiday calendar before recommending a weekday visit: normal Monday–Friday opening hours do not apply on public holidays. For a pickup task, include the location and direct map pin.
- If the booking site is inaccessible from the agent's network, use the official gov.cy information page for the collection policy and suggest booking from the owner's own device when a new application actually requires an appointment.

## Booking directly with a TOM office

Each district office books by email or phone; the official contact page lists
the current addresses: `mcw.gov.cy/mcw/rtd/rtd.nsf/contact_en`. Read the
office's booking email and phones from there on the day.

Book by email: short free-form message, attach photocopies of:
- pink slip / yellow slip — both sides
- driving licence — both sides
- foreign passport photo page

They reply with a concrete date/time. Typical wait ~2 weeks, can stretch to a month.

## Exchange a foreign licence → Cypriot

Eligible when resident 6+ months (counted from alien-book / pink-slip issue,
and 183+ days physically on island), and the issuing country is on the
exchange list — check it on the RTD site.

Documents:
- completed form **TOM 7D** (Greek form, answers may be written in English; blank at office or RTD site)
- passport + copy (entry/exit stamps are checked against a self-written list of arrival/departure dates)
- residence permit MEU1/MEU2/MEU3 + copy
- driving licence + copy (the old licence is surrendered)
- proof of 6 months residency: electricity or water bill, Social Insurance statement, bank statement, or rental contract
- translation of the licence (EN/GR) if not already Latin-transliterated — offices differ, so confirm
- photo 45×35 mm — some offices photograph on-site
- fee **€40** (under 65; free 65+)

Process: submit → temporary A4 licence issued (can insure on it) → SMS in ~15 days
→ collect new licence at TOM / Citizen Service Center (ΚΕΠ) / post office.

## Replacement / reissue (lost or stolen licence)

Booked the same way (KEP/CSC or TOM).

Bring:
- police report of the loss/theft
- residence permit (pink slip / MEU)
- passport
- €40 fee **in cash** — the 65+ exemption does **not** apply to a reissue due
  to loss/theft, so €40 is charged regardless

## Pitfalls

- Don't answer «here are the free slots» from the TOM — TOM publishes none; for
  a self-service calendar use the KEP/CSC portal above. TOM direct = email/phone.
- Pink slip must be valid (not expired / in renewal) to apply.
- Confirm the exact document list on the day — phone advice and desk requirements sometimes differ.
